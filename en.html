FAQ

Questions fréquentes

Do I need technical skills? ▼
No. If you can copy-paste a WhatsApp message, you can use Stafflo. No installation, no account required to start.
Where is my data stored? ▼
In Supabase — a secure cloud database you control. Your data never passes through our servers. Exportable at any time.
Does it work on mobile? ▼
Yes. Stafflo is mobile-first. iPhone, Android, iPad, desktop — same interface, synced everywhere.
Does it work with Airbnb and Booking? ▼
Yes. Extraction recognises Airbnb, Booking.com and WhatsApp formats — in any language.
How is it different from Guesty or Lodgify? ▼
Completely different. Guesty and Lodgify sync calendars for managers of 10+ properties. Stafflo handles the premium guest relationship — WhatsApp, meals, staff logistics, daily briefing. What the big tools don't do.
Can I manage multiple villas? ▼
The current version manages one villa per account. Multi-villa management is in development and will be available to founding members first.
