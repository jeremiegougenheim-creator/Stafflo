FAQ

Common questions

Do I need any technical skills?
None at all. If you know how to copy and paste a WhatsApp message, you know how to use Stafflo. No installation, no account required to get started.
Where is my data stored?
In Supabase — a secure cloud database that you control. Your data never passes through our servers. You can export your CRM as JSON at any time.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. Stafflo is designed mobile-first. It works perfectly on iPhone, Android, iPad and desktop, synced everywhere via Supabase.
Does it work with Airbnb and Booking?
Yes. The automatic extraction recognises Airbnb formats (email and in-app messages) and Booking.com. It also recognises direct WhatsApp conversations.
Can I manage multiple villas?
The current version manages one villa per account. Multi-villa management is in development and will be available to founding members first.
How is this different from Lodgify or Guesty?
Completely different. Lodgify and Guesty sync calendars for managers with 10+ apartments. Stafflo handles the premium guest relationship — WhatsApp, meals, logistics, staff briefings. What the big tools don't do.
