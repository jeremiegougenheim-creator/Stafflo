How it works

Three links. One source of truth.

Stafflo connects your guest, your team and you to the same base. Nobody copies one WhatsApp message into another.

The guest

Their booklet, in English or French

Wi-Fi, address, rules, menus. Before arriving, they share their time and group, and order extras at your house's prices. Nothing is paid online: you confirm.

The team

Their link, in Arabic or French

Arrivals, groups, times, meals and ordered extras — never your prices or notes. One tap: “Rooms ready”, “Report a problem”.

You

Your Espace, on your phone

Airbnb emails linked by themselves every 10 minutes, an alert for each request, the 8:30 am morning brief, and what your guests ordered.

The bridge to your team

Your team reads in Arabic. You read in English.

What the guest answered and ordered reaches the right person as is, in their language. Your team stays yours.
